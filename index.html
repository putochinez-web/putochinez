Biography

Africadançar Kizomba Champion
🥇 France  ·  🥈 World
Co-Founder of Penatchon Dance School
Founder of Flores do Semba Dance School
International dance teacher since 2011.

Puto Chinez has been teaching Kizomba and Semba for over 17 years.

Trained in France and later at the source in Angola through immersive stays devoted to both technique and culture, he is regarded as a pioneer who helped bring an authentic vision of Kizomba and Semba to France.

In 2011, he was crowned Africadançar France Champion and, in the same year, Africadançar International Vice-Champion.

A co-founder of the Penatchon school, he later founded Flores do Semba, which has trained and inspired a new generation of dancers across Europe and beyond.
